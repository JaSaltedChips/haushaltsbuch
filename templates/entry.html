Daily Entry

Record a single transaction or paste your phone log for batch review.

💡 Transfers move money between your accounts. A cash withdrawal (BANK → CASH), inter-bank transfer, or credit-card payment (BANK → CARD) are all transfers — not income or expenses.
Format (pipe-delimited, one entry per line or separated by ;): 
DATE | TYPE | AMOUNT | FROM_ACCOUNT | TO_ACCOUNT | CATEGORY | DESCRIPTION
Examples: 
2026-10-04 | EXPENSE  | 18.70  | CARD   | -      | Eating out | Lunch
2026-10-04 | INCOME   | 3200   | -      | BANK-1 | Salary     | October salary
2026-10-04 | TRANSFER | 50.00  | BANK-1 | CASH   | -          | ATM withdrawal

Use - for fields that don't apply. Account labels must match exactly (case-insensitive).
