Restoring from Backup

  1. Stop the app (Ctrl+C in the terminal).
  2. Copy your backup expense_monitor.db into the data/ folder, overwriting the existing file.
  3. Restart the app.

⚠️ Restoring overwrites your current data. Make a backup of the current database first if needed.
